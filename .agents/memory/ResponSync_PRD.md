# ResponSync

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### AI Decision Digital Twin for Predictive Disaster Response

**Version:** 1.0\
**Project Type:** Hackathon MVP (Production-Oriented)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Executive Summary

ResponSync is an AI-powered Decision Digital Twin that learns disaster
response strategies through simulation and applies those learnings
during real-world emergencies.

Unlike traditional Digital Twins that only mirror the current state of a
city, ResponSync builds a Decision Knowledge Base from simulated
disaster scenarios. During a live event, the platform compares current
environmental conditions against previous simulations, identifies
similar situations, and recommends the response strategy expected to
perform best.

## Vision

Build an AI-powered Decision Digital Twin that transforms disaster
management from reactive response to predictive decision intelligence.

## Problem

Emergency response depends on fragmented systems such as weather
services, traffic, hospitals, police, satellite monitoring, and citizen
reports. This results in slow decisions and inefficient resource
allocation.
