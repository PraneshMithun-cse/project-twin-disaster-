# Feature 05: Explainable AI (XAI) Inspector

## 📌 Feature Overview
The **Explainable AI (XAI) Inspector** provides transparent, evidence-backed explanations for every recommendation generated by the AI system. It breaks down confidence scores, supporting telemetry metrics, risks of delay, and alternative scenario trade-offs so human commanders can make fully informed decisions.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture & Component Mapping
- **Frontend Component**: [ExplainabilityModal.tsx](file:///x:/Projects/response%20sync%202/src/components/ExplainabilityModal.tsx)
- **Trigger Points**: Authority Dashboard recommendation cards (`View AI Explanation` button)
- **API Endpoint**: `POST /api/ai/explain-decision`

---

## 🧪 Manual Testing Instructions

### Step 1: Trigger the XAI Modal
1. Click the **Authority HQ** tab.
2. Locate any active AI recommendation card (e.g. *"Deploy 4 NDRF Boat Units & Station 500HP Dewatering Pumps"*).
3. Click the purple link **`View AI Explanation & Evidence`**.

### Step 2: Inspect XAI Breakdown
1. **Core Justification**: Verify the core reason (e.g. *"Rapid sluice discharge threatens ground-floor residents in Velachery South."*).
2. **Confidence Score**: Verify confidence badge ($96\%$).
3. **Evidence Data Points**:
   - `Open-Meteo Live Rain: 85mm/hr`
   - `River Basin Discharge: 1,450 m³/s`
   - `3 Verified Citizen Reports at Vijaya Nagar`
4. **Risk Explanation**: Verify risk if delayed (e.g. *"Delaying deployment by 15 minutes risks trapping ~1,400 residents."*).
5. **Alternative Risk**: Verify alternative scenario trade-off (e.g. *"Diverting boats to Kotturpuram causes higher overall casualty risk."*).

### Expected Outcome
The XAI Modal pops up cleanly with high-visibility visual metrics, telemetry citations, and clear delay risk explanations.
